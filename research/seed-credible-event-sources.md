## Trust rule

- **Confirmed**: the item comes from a ticketing platform, the promoter or venue, a bank press release or newsroom, or two independent credible outlets.
- **Rumour / soft launch**: one social post, one article, an aggregator or a music app listing. Label it as such.

# PART 2 OF THE PROMPT: KEYWORD AND DETECTION RULES (relevance first, credibility second)

Still research and specification only. Do not write bot code. Produce the two deliverables listed at the end of this part.

## The order of checks (important)
When a piece of content is found anywhere on the internet, run the checks in THIS order:

**Step 1. Relevance gate (does the content match what I need?)**
The content passes only if BOTH of these are true:
- It matches the event-intent keywords (below), meaning it announces, teases, or gives sale details for a live event.
- The event venue or city is in India. The artist can be from anywhere (a foreign artist playing Bengaluru passes; an Indian artist playing Abu Dhabi fails).
If either test fails, drop it silently. Do not judge credibility yet.

**Step 2. Extract the facts.**
Pull only what the content states: artist or event name, city, venue, event date, sale start (presale and general), early bird pricing, card or bank offers, ticket link, source URL and publish time. Leave a field blank if it isn't stated. Never infer or invent.

**Step 3. Credibility check (only for content that passed Step 1).**
Look up the source in `sources.json` and classify it:
- Tier 1: official ticketer, promoter, venue, bank press release or artist channel.
- Tier 2: established trade or news outlet.
- Tier 3 or unknown: aggregator, blog, fan page, anonymous or unrecognised social account, or any source not in the list.

Then label the item:
- **CONFIRMED**: Tier 1 source, OR two independent Tier 1/Tier 2 sources agree on the key facts.
- **LIKELY**: one Tier 2 source, no official source yet. Tag it "awaiting official confirmation".
- **UNVERIFIED**: the best source is below Tier 2 (the "too raw" case). Still send the alert so soft launches and early leaks aren't missed, but attach this warning: "Non-credible or unverified source. Please check the official ticketer or promoter before acting."

Also: if sources disagree on a date, venue or price, show the conflict instead of picking one. If an official source appears later, send an upgrade notice ("now confirmed by <source>").

## Keywords to build (propose full lists, I will edit them)
Produce lists for each group below. Include common spelling variants, hyphenation (pre-sale, presale), Hinglish and regional-language terms where relevant.

1. **Announcement intent**: announces, announced, tour, India tour, concert, gig, live in, to perform, set to perform, headline, headlining, debut in India, returns to India, comeback, festival, lineup, line-up, "dates out", "city list".
2. **Ticket and sale intent**: tickets, ticket sale, "tickets go live", "on sale", "sale opens", presale, pre-sale, early bird, general sale, registration open, waitlist, "notify me", phase 1 / phase 2, tier pricing, "limited tickets", "sold out".
3. **Card and bank offers**: card names and issuers (for example Kotak, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, SBI Card, Amex, Visa, RuPay, Mastercard, Pixel, Solitaire, White, White Reserve), "exclusive access", "cardholders", "priority access", "x% off".
4. **Event types**: concert, live show, tour, music festival, DJ set, club night, stand-up, comedy tour, theatre, musical, classical or ghazal evening, regional-language shows.
5. **Soft-launch and teaser signals**: "save the date", "something big is coming", "announcement soon", "registration opens", "join the waitlist", countdown or teaser posts from official accounts.
6. **India geography**: all major cities and many tier-2 cities, states, and a venue-name list per city (use the venues section of the source file and expand). A match here is required to pass the gate. If a venue name is ambiguous, require the city to match too.
7. **Artist watchlist**: (a) a list I manage and can edit, and (b) a suggested starter list of global and Indian artists that frequently tour India, clearly marked as a suggestion for me to review. A watchlist hit raises priority, but an artist NOT on the list can still pass the gate if the intent keywords and an India venue are present.
8. **Negative filters (drop or handle separately)**: past-event recaps and reviews, fan photo posts, giveaways and contests, resale or scalper listings, events whose venue is outside India, and generic news with no event. **Cancellations, postponements and venue or date changes are NOT dropped.** Mark them high priority.
